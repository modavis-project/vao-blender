# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to making participation in this project a respectful, harassment-free
experience regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience, education,
socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, specific, and constructive.
- Welcome questions and differing levels of experience.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Respect privacy, attribution, cultural context, rights, and embargoes.
- Accept responsibility, apologize, and repair harm when appropriate.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, sustained disruption, doxxing, or publishing private information
without permission are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through the
contact route in [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md). Maintainers will protect reporter
privacy as far as practicable, investigate proportionately, and may edit or
remove contributions, issue a warning, impose a temporary restriction, or ban a
participant. Retaliation against a reporter is prohibited.

This code applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the
project. Enforcement decisions may be appealed privately to a maintainer who was
not the original decision-maker when one is available.
